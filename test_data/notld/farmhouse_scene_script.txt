NIGHT OF THE LIVING DEAD
Screenplay by George A. Romero & John A. Russo (1968)

SCENE SEQUENCE: THE FARMHOUSE SIEGE (Minutes 25:00 - 45:00)

[SCENE 12: INT. FARMHOUSE - LIVING ROOM - NIGHT]
BEN enters, breathing heavily. He bolts the heavy wooden front door and immediately turns to assess the perimeter.
BARBRA sits huddled on the sofa in the corner of the room, shivering in shock, her trench coat pulled tight around her. Her eyes are glazed over.

BEN
(firmly, to Barbra)
We have to board this place up. Everything. Every door, every window. There are more of them out there.

Barbra does not respond. Her gaze remains locked on the floor. Her shoes are kicked off or missing by the sofa.

Ben moves swiftly across the room. He assesses the heavy oak dining table. With brute force, he flips the table onto its side and begins wrenching off the legs and tearing the wooden planks apart using an iron tire iron and hammer.

BEN (CONT'D)
They're afraid of fire. I saw it out there. If we get enough wood, we can keep them back.

Ben carries several raw lumber planks over to the front door frame. He retrieves heavy construction nails and drives the boards horizontally across the door and window frames to form a reinforced barricade.

[SCENE 13: INT. FARMHOUSE - LIVING ROOM & FIREPLACE - NIGHT]
Ben moves to the fireplace hearth. On the mantel and hearth floor, he searches for combustibles.
He spots a metal rectangular container labeled "CHARCOAL LIGHTER" fluid.
Ben grabs the lighter fluid container with his right hand. 

BEN
This'll do it.

He takes one of the splintered table legs, wraps torn curtain fabric tightly around the top, and drenches the cloth in charcoal starter fluid.
He sets the lighter fluid container down by the fireside chair to prepare the ignition.
Using matches, he strikes a flame and ignites the torch. The cloth bursts into a violent, roaring orange flame, casting deep flickering shadows across the living room walls.

Ben kicks open the front door barricade slightly, thrusts the flaming torch outward, and hurls ignited wood onto the porch to drive back the approaching ghouls.

[SCENE 14: INT. FARMHOUSE - HALLWAY & CLOSET - NIGHT]
Holding a flashlight, Ben moves cautiously through the central hallway toward the under-stair storage closet.
He pulls the closet door open. The interior is cluttered with coats, linens, and domestic storage.
On the upper wooden shelf sits a collection of boxes, shoes, and household supplies.
Ben rummages through the shelf. He uncovers a Winchester lever-action repeating rifle and a cardboard shoe box filled with loose ammunition.

BEN
Rifle. With shells.

He pulls the lever down to inspect the chamber, verifies the feed mechanism, and loads cartridges into the side gate. He grips the rifle firmly, holding the barrel angled upward as he turns back to the living room.

[SCENE 15: INT. FARMHOUSE - UPSTAIRS HALLWAY & BEDROOM - NIGHT]
Ben heads up the stairs carrying hammer, nails, and reinforcement planks to secure the second-floor windows.
The upstairs hallway is dark and narrow.
Ben positions horizontal wooden planks across the window frames, hammering each side securely into the plaster and wood molding.
As he approaches the top landing, he notices a closed bedroom door.
He pushes it open. Inside, illuminated by moonlight, lies a mutilated, partially devoured corpse on the bed.
Ben steps back, nauseated and alarmed. He backs quickly down the stairs, double-checking his rifle.

[SCENE 16: INT. FARMHOUSE - LIVING ROOM & CELLAR DOOR - NIGHT]
Downstairs, Barbra remains motionless on the couch. 
Suddenly, a noise emanates from beneath the floorboards.
The heavy wooden door leading down to the cellar begins to rattle.
Ben raises the Winchester rifle, aiming directly at the cellar latch.

BEN
Hold it right there! Come out with your hands up!

The cellar door creaks open. HARRY COOPER emerges cautiously, blinking under the harsh room light, his face beaded with sweat.
Behind him follow his wife HELEN COOPER, their sick daughter KAREN, and young teenagers TOM and JUDY.

HARRY
(defiant, aggressive)
Who are you? What are you doing up here? It's not safe up here! We have to lock ourselves in the cellar!

BEN
(angry, lowering the rifle slightly)
You were down there the whole time? You heard us banging and screaming out here and you didn't lift a finger to help?

HARRY
My family is down there! The cellar is the only safe place in this house! There's one door—we can hold the cellar!

BEN
You lock yourself in that cellar and you're in a death trap! If they get into this house, they'll surround you and starve you out! Up here we can see them coming, we have windows, we have weapons, and we have fire!

The argument escalates violently. The single key light casts harsh shadows across Harry's face as he glares at Ben, refusing to cooperate.
Tom steps forward between them to intervene and propose a compromise.
